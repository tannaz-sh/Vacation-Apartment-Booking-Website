# Product requirements

## Confirmed
RF Sauerland Immobilien offers short-term holiday apartment stays in Winterberg, Germany. German responsive website, custom code. Client owns hosting/domain/Smoobu/third-party costs. Planning target: 30 working days plus 30 calendar days post-delivery technical support; dependencies and approvals affect dates.

## Public scope and acceptance
- Home, apartment listing and details, about, contact, FAQ; accessible mobile/tablet/desktop navigation.
- Listing filters marketing capacity/amenities only, never claims live availability.
- Details include approved gallery/alt text, guest capacity, amenities, arrival/departure, house rules and a correctly mapped provider booking entry.
- Smoobu Booking Engine owns dates, prices, minimum stays, fees, payment and confirmation. No success page without genuine provider confirmation.
- Contact validates on client/server, enforces spam controls, delivers to verified operator inbox and distinguishes failure from delivery.
- FAQ helper returns only approved answers and acknowledges unknowns.
- Approved Impressum/privacy/booking/cancellation text before release.
- 404/loading/empty/error/provider-unavailable states; metadata, sitemap and robots.

## Administration acceptance
Established authenticated CMS for marketing content, Smoobu ID mapping, photos/alt/order/featured, pages/FAQ, drafts/preview/publish and SEO/slugs. Main administrator initially; editor only if needed. Anonymous and unauthorized users cannot read drafts or mutate content. Backup and restore rehearsal required. Reservations stay in Smoobu.

## Unknowns
Apartment count/content/photos/rights, operator legal details, domain/host/budget, CMS approval, operator email and mail transport, approved FAQ and policies, Smoobu configuration/URLs/IDs, test procedure. Guest booking overview is conditional on verified existing Smoobu capability.

## Unconfirmed, not publishable
EUR 99/night Dec–Mar (year/dates unknown), two-night minimum, EUR 69/59/49 cleaning tiers, 14-day free cancellation and 50%/90% tiers, payment methods. Tiered cleaning support unverified. Taxes, mandatory fees and other seasonal rates unresolved.
